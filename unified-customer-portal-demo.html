Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How does ADAB Marketplace work?

ADAB connects you with verified local sellers. Browse products, add to cart (even from multiple sellers), checkout once, and each seller ships your items independently.

How do returns work?

Go to My Orders → select the order → Request Return. Choose your reason. The seller will arrange pickup within 48 hours. Refund is processed once the item is received.

How do reward points work?

Earn ~1 point per ₹100 spent. Use up to 50% of order value. 1 point = ₹1. Points expire after 12 months.

Can I order from multiple sellers?

Yes! Cart groups items by seller. Each seller prepares and ships separately. One checkout, one payment.
