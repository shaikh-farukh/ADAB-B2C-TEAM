FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

Search and browse by topic — credit, orders, products, delivery, points, and more

How does credit work between stores?

You set credit days (7–60), min order, max ₹ per store, and max number of shops that can buy on your credit. When you buy, each seller shows their terms. You can owe up to 5 shops at once (configurable), max ₹25,000 per seller by default.

What is ADAB credit line vs shop credit?

Shop credit: You owe the seller directly — they wait for payment. ADAB credit: ADAB pays the seller instantly; you repay ADAB in 14 days. Apply under Money → Apply for Credit.

What happens if I exceed max shops on credit?

At checkout, ADAB blocks credit payment if you already owe the maximum number of shops. Pay one seller first, or use UPI/cash/ADAB line instead.

App orders vs store orders — distance?

App orders only from customers inside your zone (e.g. 10 km) — bike/van. Store orders from anywhere — Porter, Delhivery, freight, export.

How do I filter and export orders?

Use search + status + delivery type filters on App Orders and Store Orders pages. Order History has date, type, and status filters with CSV export.

What are partner brand packs vs FMCG catalog?

Partner packs = products other shops created as their brand — you resell with your price. FMCG catalog = national barcodes (Tata, Amul) from master list.

Own brand vs loose vs food products?

Own brand = your label. Loose = sold by weight. National pack = scanned FMCG. Food = ready dishes with FSSAI.

How do I change my customer delivery zone?

Go to Delivery Zones → set radius 3–20 km. Only customers inside the green circle on the map can order from your app listing.

How is far store freight tracked?

Use Freight Tracking for AWB numbers — Delhivery, Porter truck, export shipments. Store orders have no distance limit.

When do I earn reward points?

Earn when selling (1–3 pts per ₹100) AND buying (0.5–1 pt per ₹100). Redeem up to 50% on next purchase. See Points ledger with SELL/BUY/USED tags.

What coupon types can I create?

Product, shop-wide, category, or delivery coupons — for customers or other stores. Set min order and expiry in Coupons & Offers.

When does money reach my bank?

Customer payments settle next business day by 11:30 PM. Credit from stores is guaranteed by ADAB on the due date. Withdraw anytime from Money & Credit.

How do I apply for more buying credit?

Go to Apply for Credit in the sidebar. Submit requested limit, reason, and sales data. Approval usually takes 1–2 business days if GSTIN and store health are good.

What is in the 11-step store setup?

Profile, store details, hours, who buys from you, credit you give (with max shops), delivery zone, bank, documents, ADAB buying credit, fleet, and review.

Can I sell internationally?

Yes — enable export in setup and upload IEC certificate under Certificates. Export orders appear in Store Orders → Export tab.

How to add products

Use Product Studio for own brand, loose, packed, or food items. Set who can buy: customers, other stores, or both.

How credit works

Buy stock now, pay in 14 days at 0% interest. Apply for higher limits from Money & Credit section.

Reward points

Earn 1 point per ₹100 customer sale, 2 points per ₹100 store sale. Redeem up to 50% when buying stock.

Customer delivery

Offer Fast (under 1hr), Same-day (2-4hr), Normal slots, or Pickup. Partner riders available as fallback.

Store-to-store delivery

Always uses truck/freight: Porter, Delhivery, or export freight. Never bike delivery for bulk.

Export selling

Upload IEC certificate, list products for international buyers. ADAB handles customs documentation.

POS billing

Bill walk-in customers at counter. Sales count toward reward points and daily revenue.

Bulk CSV import

Upload CSV with columns: name, barcode, price, stock, type. Progress bar shows import status.

Mass price update

Select products or category, apply percentage increase/decrease or set flat price.

GSTIN compliance

Verified GSTIN shown in header badge. Required for invoicing and credit applications.

FSSAI for food

Required for grocery and food items. Upload in Certificates section.

Settlement flow

Customer pays → ADAB holds → Bank transfer next day by 11:30 PM.

Credit khata

Track who owes you and who you owe. Send reminders and settle from Money section.

Coupons & offers

Create percentage, flat, or free delivery coupons for customers or stores.

What to Sell AI

Recommendations based on local demand, profit margins, and nearby store buying requests.
