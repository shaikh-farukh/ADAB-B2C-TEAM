How is ADAB different from other apps?

You order from real local shops — kirana, fashion stores, dairy — not a central warehouse. Each shop sets delivery time, offers, and products.

How fast is delivery?

Express 14–45 mins, same-day by evening, scheduled slots, or free pickup from the shop. Options depend on each store.

Can I order from multiple shops?

Yes. Cart groups items by shop. Each shop prepares and delivers separately when needed.

How do reward points work?

Earn ~1 pt per ₹100 spent. Redeem up to 50% at checkout. 1 pt = ₹1 off.

Can I chat with a shop?

Yes — message the store about products, order changes, or delivery from the shop page or Messages in your profile.

Returns & refunds?

Request within 7 days. Shop picks up item; refund to UPI or wallet once approved.
